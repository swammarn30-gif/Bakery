# Reference UI notes — swammarn30.netlify.app

The authenticated reference uses a very light gray page background with a compact dark navy header. The header has a small boxed “B” brand mark, Bakery ERP title/subtitle, business-date control, and a compact sign-out avatar/action. Navigation is a horizontal row of pill-like buttons directly below the header: Dashboard, Item Dashboard, Purchase, Production, Packaging, Sale, Reports, and More. The active tab is dark navy with white text; inactive tabs are white with thin gray borders and small icons.

The dashboard content is dense but calm: a simple page title and subtitle, followed by KPI cards in a responsive grid. Cards are mostly white with soft rounded corners and subtle shadows; the highlighted KPI uses a dark navy fill with white text. Accent strokes/colors distinguish damage and sales metrics. A large white low-stock panel follows with a title row, warning icon, right-aligned alert count, and centered empty-state message.

Design direction to adapt: preserve the Bakery ERP’s mobile-first operational tables, but borrow the reference’s navy/white/light-gray palette, compact horizontal navigation, pill tabs, small uppercase/secondary labels, soft card elevation, KPI emphasis, and generous empty states. Do not copy exact text, markup, or branding.
